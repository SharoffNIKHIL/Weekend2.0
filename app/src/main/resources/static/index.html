        Agents

        Pick who answers in Chat, give an agent your own instructions and conditions, or connect another agent.

          Create your own agent

          Your instructions are added after Weekend's safety rules, which always win.

          Connect another agent

          Weekend talks to other agents over HTTPS ("weekend-agent/1"). Every message you send them waits for your yes, and they get a token to message you back.

          Remote agents are switched off. Add the agent's host to WEEKEND_AGENT_ALLOWED_HOSTS after a security checkpoint.
